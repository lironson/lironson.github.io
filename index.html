    Golf Swing P-Positions

    Analyzes your golf swing across the 10 P‑positions. You upload your swing positions and it draws guide lines with coaching tips.

    trysource

  3. 03

    Steel Size Rolodex

    A quick lookup for structural steel sections, with metric and imperial sizes. It finds the closest match to whatever you type and draws the section with a banana for scale, of course.

    trysource

  4. 04

    Have You Eaten Yet?

    Turns your trip's food photos into a ranked guide for friends heading to the same place. Drag the dishes into order or settle it with a 1v1 bracket, then print or share it.
