ronson.

a quiet system. mostly paper, a little ink, one orange dot.

01 · rules

  1. empty space is the main material. when in doubt, remove it.
  2. one typeface does the work. mono is for small notes only.
  3. lines are hairlines. ticks at the ends, sometimes.
  4. the orange is the dot in the wordmark. nothing else, usually.
  5. lowercase, plain, a little dry.
